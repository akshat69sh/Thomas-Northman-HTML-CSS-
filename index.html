About Thomas

Thomas started his own practice in 2012 and prior to
that, worked in various law firms in and around San
Francisco. He also formerly worked at the United
States Tax Court and in the Tax Division of the Justice
Department, where he helped prosecute a number of
sophisticated, international tax shelters.
